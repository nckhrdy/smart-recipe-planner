Spinach & Tomato Frittata

Fluffy baked eggs with wilted spinach and blistered cherry tomatoes — a fridge-clear-out classic.

10m
Prep
20m
Cook
30m
Total
Cook timer
Tap to set a countdown
20:00minutes
Ingredients
Serves
4
Large eggs6
Fresh spinach2 cups
Cherry tomatoes1 cup, halved
Yellow onion½, diced
Garlic2 cloves
Cheddar, grated½ cup
Olive oil1 tbsp
Salt & pepperto taste
Method
1

Heat the oven to 190°C (375°F).

2

Sauté onion and garlic in olive oil over medium heat until soft, about 4 minutes.

3

Add spinach and cook until wilted, ~2 minutes. Stir through the cherry tomatoes.

4

Whisk the eggs with salt and pepper; pour over the vegetables in an oven-safe pan.

5

Scatter cheddar on top and cook on the stove for 2 minutes until the edges set.

6

Bake for 12–15 minutes until golden and just set. Slice and serve warm.
